I am a first-year PhD candidate in the **HKUST NLP Group** and a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)** (2024–Present), supervised by **Professor Junxian He**. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. My research interests include LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), LLM truthfulness, and interpretability.

## Skills and Research Areas

The recorded profile identifies the following research skills and technical topics, including topics represented in my publications:

- Natural language processing and machine learning research.
- LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, including large-scale synthesis of verifiable reasoning data for logical reasoning and beyond.
- Hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), including chart understanding and the perception bottleneck of VLMs.
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability, including inner-representation perspectives and inner-representation alerts for hallucination mitigation.
- Multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suites for foundation models.
- Composition of parameter-efficient modules with arithmetic operations.
